# ARENA ROUND 10 — RED (Claude) AAR

**Result: RED WINS at t=561s (9:21).** Series: Rusher 6, Boomer 3, 1 draw.

## Match length vs the 10-20 min target
9:21 — still SHORT of the 10-20 minute target, but nearly double R9's 5:24.
The 5000g mines did exactly what they were meant to: they bought a real mid-game.
The kill did not come from a 5-minute dive; it came at 7:40+ from a fully teched
army after both sides had spent 3 minutes building tech and expanding.

## T2/T3 content actually fielded — and did it decide anything?
- **Keep** (4:00): yes. Bought the second hero slot and the Sanctum.
- **Priestess**: fielded, died at 8:28, revived for 250g. Marginal — she kept
  drifting out of the squad and contributed little. Autocast Heal at 2 enemies
  did not keep her with the line.
- **Arcane Sanctum / 3 Sorcerers**: fielded. Slow autocast was live in the base
  assault. Hard to credit decisively — BLUE's field army never massed against me.
- **Blacksmith**: Attack 1 and Armor 1 both completed before the push. Cheap,
  retroactive, clearly worth it. This is the best gold-per-value T2 purchase.
- **Castle / Knights / Gryphons: NEVER REACHED.** Not because gold was short —
  I finished with 1539g banked and peaked at 2900 — but because **lumber** was
  the hard cap all game (sat at 40-180 for the whole midgame). Castle costs 240
  lumber. That is the finding: at 5000g mines, gold stops being the tech gate and
  lumber becomes it, so T3 stays out of reach even in a won game.
- Verdict on the round's question: **the rusher line still wins, but it now wins
  as a T2 timing push rather than a T1 dive.** I banked Keep + Blacksmith +
  Sanctum and attacked at 7:14 instead of 5:00. That is the honest adaptation.

## Fog and ford play
- Fog worked against me hard: I had zero enemy information until 4:51 (their
  scouting Raider found me first), and I never noticed BLUE abandoning their
  dead main for the NW ford mine until I was standing in the rubble of their base
  at 8:17. Killing a base and finding out it wasn't the whole faction is exactly
  what fog should do, and it did.
- Fords: I expanded onto the SE ford mine (60,-60) at 4:35 and walled it with a
  Tower — that hall carried my economy after the main died and was never attacked.
  BLUE mirrored me onto the NW ford. Both flank fords became the second base for
  the side nearest them, which is good map design.
- My worst play was walking mining workers to the NW ford mine (-60,60) at 8:00
  without checking who owned it. BLUE's expansion was sitting on it. Lost 7
  workers for nothing.

## Top 3 remaining complaints
1. **`forage` posture has no leash and will suicide into a defended base.** At
   4:50 it took my entire army from (30,30) to (-58,-64) and fed it to a Tower
   one unit at a time — the exact "trickle" failure the brief warns against, done
   BY the doctrine system rather than by me. Forage should respect a max radius
   from its muster point, or at minimum not attack-move into enemy static
   defense. This cost me 6 Footmen and was my single biggest loss of the game.
2. **Lumber, not gold, is now the tech gate — and there is no lumber lever.**
   Mines went 3500 -> 5000; trees did not change. I ended with 1539 unspent gold
   and could not afford a Castle (240 lumber). If T3 is meant to be reachable,
   either trees need more yield, or T3 needs a gold-weighted price. Right now
   "does the commander use T3 when the economy allows" is unanswerable because
   the economy never allows it.
3. **Squad cohesion silently breaks for heroes.** My Priestess was in squad 1 the
   whole match and spent most of it 40-100 units away from the squad centroid
   (at one point at (-74,48) while the squad fought at (-55,-55)). She died alone.
   A push squad that "gathers before pressing on" should gather its heroes too —
   or `why` should tell me which policy pulled her out, and it didn't.
