# Round 10 — BLUE (Boomer) AAR

**Result: LOSS by surrender at t=563s (9:23).** Series now Rusher 6, Boomer 3, 1 draw.

## Match length
9:23. Below the 10-20 minute target, but the closest a Boomer game has come. The
mine change (5000g) did buy a real mid-game: I reached Keep at 3:30, had a Blacksmith,
Sanctum, Workshop, both research ladders started, and an expansion hall standing at 7:23.
None of that existed in R9. The game did not end because the economy ran out — it ended
because I lost every fight.

- 0:35 late start (workers idle at first snapshot), harvest + 4 workers ordered.
- 1:25-2:50 Barracks, 2 Farms, Champion, Workshop. Spearman/Archer trickle.
- 2:50 Keep upgrade started; 3:30 Keep complete.
- 3:40-4:00 Blacksmith + Sanctum + first Tower. Weapon Smithing 1 started.

  Armor 1 completes. I now own the map's only remaining gold.

  while my whole army was escorting the expansion 130 units away. Barracks, Blacksmith,
  Sanctum, Tower razed in ~20s; Keep and Workshop dead by 8:15.

- 8:55 THIRD ATTACK: 19 units arrive at the expansion. Tower, TownHall, all workers
  and the whole army gone in ~15 seconds. Hero dead at level 2.
- 9:23 No hall, no hero, 4 workers, 2195 unspendable gold. Surrendered.

## What I fielded from T2/T3
Keep, Blacksmith (Weapon Smithing 1 + Armor Plating 1 both completed), Arcane Sanctum,
Sorcerers (3 trained, all died within seconds of spawning, never cast a meaningful Slow),
Workshop, one Raider. Never reached Castle, never fielded a Knight or a Gryphon.
**Nothing at T2 decided anything.** The +1/+1 was live for the last two fights and was
invisible next to a 2:1 unit count. The Sorcerers died before their 8-radius mattered
because they spawned into a base already being overrun.

## Ford fortification — partial, and it did not hold
Two towers finished (one at the main approach, one at the NW ford). The main tower did
real work in the 4:47 defense. The ford tower died in about 8 seconds to 19 units. Two
more towers were ordered at 8:55 and the workers assigned to them were killed before the
foundations went down. So: fortification finally happened, and the answer is that ONE
tower is not a fortification. The R9 complaint is answered and replaced by a new one.

## Fog play
Better than R9 but still thin. I built the Raider at 4:00 and it did exactly what the
brief promised — it found an 8-Footman push a full 20 seconds before it hit me, which
is why the first attack was survivable. Then I converted it to a forage squad and it died
alone, and I never rebuilt eyes. Both later attacks arrived as complete surprises: the
15-stack hit my main while I was staring at the NW ford, and I only learned their army
size when it was already inside my base. I also never once saw their base. I ended the
game having scouted zero enemy production buildings except a remembered Blacksmith ghost.

## Top 3 remaining complaints
1. **Army split is instantly fatal and there is no warning.** The whole game turned on
   one decision at 7:23: escort the expansion with the entire army. Between deciding and
   the counterattack landing there was no signal, and once it landed, the 14-second march
   home was the entire game. Escorting an expansion and defending a main are 130 units
   apart on this map and the doctrine system offers nothing between them — a defend
   posture does not react to a base that is out of its radius.
2. **CallToArms is unreliable at the moment it matters.** I cast it twice successfully at
   the Keep and it clearly helped at 4:47. At the expansion it was rejected with
   `caster 4294969508 is not a hero or an own ability building` — a plain TownHall, which
   the catalog lists as a CallToArms caster. Whatever the real gate is (tier? recently
   built?), it fired the one time I did not need it and refused the one time I did.
3. **Gold is not convertible fast enough to matter.** I surrendered on 2195 gold and 370
   lumber. From 8:00 onward I was richer than the opponent every single second and had no
   way to turn that into units faster than one Barracks queue. The economic win condition
   the new mine numbers create — I owned the map's LAST gold mine from 5:24 to the end —
   is worthless if the conversion rate from banked gold to standing army is slower than
   the enemy's ability to walk across the map. A second Barracks was the obvious answer
   and I never built one; that is my error, but the game also never punished the opponent
   for having zero income, and it should have.

## Honest self-assessment
I fixed the R9 errors and lost worse. I scouted, I got the hero up before expanding, I
built towers, I reached T2 with research. What I did not fix is the thing that actually
kills the boomer line: I keep spending on buildings when the opponent is spending on
units, and every fight in this match was fought at roughly half their unit count. The
opponent played straight tempo — three pushes, each bigger than the last, never a wasted
building — and it beat a technically correct boom by simple arithmetic. Boom-into-tech
does not beat tempo here yet, because tech is priced in the same currency as army and
arrives too late to pay for itself.
