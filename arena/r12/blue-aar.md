# ARENA R12 — BLUE (Boomer creed) After-Action Report

**Result:** LOSS by surrender at t=567s (9:27). Series: Rusher 7, Boomer 3, 1 draw — four straight losses.
**Length vs 10-20min target:** 9:27 — under the window again, and again decided by an army-vs-army collapse, not by economy.

## Did T3 arrive inside the window? YES.
- Barracks ~1:25, Keep started 2:49 / complete **3:29**, Workshop 3:15, Blacksmith + Sanctum ~4:00,
  Sorcerers from 4:30, **Castle complete 6:05**, Weapon Smithing 1+2 and Armor Plating 1 all finished by 7:00.
- The tech hypothesis is answered affirmatively: with deliberate lumber allocation, T3 lands at ~6 minutes comfortably.

## Lumber allocation (the assigned experiment)
- Split from the first minute: 3 gold / 2 lumber, growing to roughly 9 gold / 5-6 lumber by 4:00, on 15 workers.
- It worked completely. Lumber never gated a single building: TownHall->Keep (160l), Workshop (100l),
  Blacksmith (80l), Sanctum (130l), Keep->Castle (240l), three research levels (200l), 5 farms, 3 towers —
  the whole ~1000-lumber ladder was paid on schedule, and I still ended the game holding **1050 unspent lumber**.
- The R10 failure mode (unspendable gold) inverted: this game I died with unspendable *lumber* and an
  exhausted gold mine. The real resource curve I misread was the **mine**, not the tree.

## The actual cause of death: gold exhaustion + a lost map
- My home mine (5000) ran dry at ~7:50. I had 15 workers eating it and never scouted for a second income
  until it was already at 900.
- The enemy had already expanded to the SE mine (TownHall at (62,-52)) by ~7:30. I found this only by
  reading the mine counter dropping on a mine I was not touching — my only scout (a Raider) died at 5:11
  and I never replaced it. That is the same "keep eyes alive" lesson, failed again.
- I sent 3 workers to the last virgin mine at (-60,60). All three died on arrival to unseen enemies. A
  second attempt with a 6-unit escort was in transit when the enemy hit my base with 19 units.

## Second production building: YES — and it did not matter
- Workshop went up at 3:15 (Raiders/Catapults/Gryphons). A second Barracks was planned but never built
  because gold went to Castle + research + towers instead. So I had two production *buildings* but only
  ever one that made line units — the R10 miss was only half-corrected.

## Targeted Slow: essentially unused
- I trained 3 Sorcerers and set autocast at 2 enemies. The first enemy attack (6:34) killed all three
  inside 10 seconds along with the rest of a 9-unit army. I never observed a Slow landing on a charge,
  and never got a second caster group up. The tool never got to be tested.

## Timeline
- 0:58 start, 5 workers. 1:25 Barracks. 2:14 Farm 2. 3:29 Keep. 3:15 Workshop. 4:00 Blacksmith+Sanctum.
- 4:27 Weapon Smithing 1. 5:11 Raider scout dies mid — last vision I ever had.
- 6:05 Castle (T3). 6:34 enemy hits with 16 units: my entire 9-unit army dies in ~12s; 3 towers repel the remnant.
- 7:00-8:30 rebuilding army piecemeal while home mine dies; two failed expansions to (-60,60), 4 workers lost.
- 9:02 enemy returns with 19 units (Knights + 8 Archers + Hero + Priestess + Sorcerers). Three towers,
  the Barracks and the Sanctum fall in 20 seconds. 9:27 surrender with Castle at 940/2200.

## Top 3 complaints
1. **I boomed the tech tree and never boomed the map.** T3 at 6:05 is a fine number attached to one mine.
   The enemy's second TownHall was worth more than my Castle. Expansion is not a late luxury; it is the boom.
2. **The army was never a thing, only a residue.** Everything I built between 3:00 and 6:00 was a building.
   At 6:34 I had 9 units vs 16 and lost all 9 for nothing. Research that multiplies an army that does not
   exist multiplies zero, and three towers do not replace a field force.
3. **I split at the worst possible moment.** At 8:30 I sent my entire 6-unit army north to escort an
   expansion while 4 enemy Knights were already inside my base — I traded my defense for a hall I could
   not even afford (375 gold vs 385 cost). That single decision turned a bad game into a finished one.
